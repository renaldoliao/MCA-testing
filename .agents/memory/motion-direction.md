---
name: Motion direction
description: The user's durable motion-design goal for the customer website and staff dashboard.
---

Use polished, premium-feeling animation across the customer website and staff dashboard. Keep transitions short, purposeful, and seamless; avoid distracting ambient motion and respect reduced-motion preferences.

Make interaction feedback clearly perceptible, not barely visible: tab indicators should travel between selections and tab content should animate on each change; button presses should feel tactile without becoming bouncy or slow.

**Why:** The user has repeatedly emphasized animation quality across both surfaces and said the first interaction pass was still too subtle.

**How to apply:** Cover the full experience—not just controls—with page/section transitions, photography and cards, navigation, ordering, overlays, and dashboard. For actions, combine a clear press/release with a brief highlight and visible value-change feedback; keep it purposeful, not bouncy.
